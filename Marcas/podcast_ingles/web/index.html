About

Social Networks: The Price of Free Data Protection is a podcast about a question many of us ignore: if social media is free, how do these companies make money? Today, apps like Instagram and TikTok collect our likes, our location and the time we spend on each post, and they use this data to show us targeted ads. In our episode, we explain how this tracking works, why we are the product, and what we can do to protect our privacy. It is an important topic right now, because we spend hours online every day and our digital footprint keeps growing.
